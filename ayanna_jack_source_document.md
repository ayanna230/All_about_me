### TransIQ (flagship project)

A Jamaican public-transport intelligence app. Ayanna served as **ML Engineer for Team TransIQ at the Intellibus Hackathon 2026** in Kingston (a 24-hour hackathon). She built and deployed the TransIQ **ML API** (Python/Flask) with six live models covering ETA prediction, fare prediction, traffic congestion, best departure time, route reliability, and safety scoring, exposing endpoints (health check, single-route prediction, multi-plan route generation, transport hub listing) publicly via ngrok during the event.

Users enter a start location, destination, budget, and travel time; the app returns three optimized route plans — **Fastest, Cheapest, Balanced** — across route taxis, JUTC buses, Knutsford Express, JUTA, and Uber, island-wide. Ayanna finished her piece ahead of the rest of the team and spent the remaining hackathon time helping teammates with theirs.
